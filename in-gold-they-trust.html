About This Report

This report is a revised version of my undergraduate thesis, The Impact of Fluctuations in the Price of Gold on Education in Tanzania. It uses the same data to answer the same question, but compares households within the same region and year.

Using three rounds of the Tanzania National Panel Survey, I find that the ninety-one percent rise in the price of gold between 2008 and 2012 is associated with a 13.9 percentage point decline in enrollment among fourteen- to seventeen-year-olds living within thirty kilometers of an artisanal gold site. However, this estimate is not statistically significant. In short, I do not find strong evidence that the gold boom reduced enrollment, although a large effect cannot be ruled out. Section 8 of the report describes each change from the original analysis and how it affects the results.
